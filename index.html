How to Buy

Four steps. Takes about two minutes if your wallet's ready.

Get a Wallet

Download MetaMask or any Ethereum-compatible wallet. It's free and takes five minutes to set up.

Add ETH

Buy Ethereum on Coinbase, Kraken, or wherever you prefer, then send it to your wallet address.

Go to Uniswap

Open uniswap.org, connect your wallet, and switch to Ethereum mainnet. Paste the Optimus on ETH contract address into the search bar.

Swap & Confirm

Enter how much ETH you want to spend. OPTIMUS has 0% buy and sell tax, so Uniswap's Auto slippage works. If the swap fails, raise it to 2–3%. Approve the transaction, and you're in. Welcome to Optimus on ETH.

Heads up: sending OPTIMUS from one wallet to another carries a fixed 9% tax, so buy straight into the wallet you'll hold in.

FAQ

The questions people actually ask before they ape in.

Is it a honeypot?

No. The contract source is published and verified on Etherscan, contract ownership has been renounced (owner is the zero address), and people trade in and out on Uniswap every day. Always check for yourself before you buy.

What are the taxes?

0% buy tax and 0% sell tax. Wallet-to-wallet transfers carry a fixed 9% tax that can't be removed because ownership is renounced.

Who controls the liquidity?

Nobody. The OPTIMUS/WETH liquidity sits in the Uniswap V2 pool, and essentially 100% of its LP tokens are burned — sent to the 0x…dEaD address, with the remaining 1,000 wei held by the zero address as Uniswap's permanent minimum liquidity. Burned LP can't be withdrawn by anyone. See the LP holders on Etherscan.

What's the deal with the CTO?

There's no single founder. The community took over organically when the original direction stalled. It's a collective — which is exactly why nobody can just walk away with the project.
